ytmanager uploader

ytmanager uploader is a private tool that its owner uses to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels: Why Nations Last, Inside Everything and Lær norsk Truong. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

With the owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, add them to playlists, and update channel branding.
